What a Marketing Design Project Can Include

Every project is scoped to your campaign and designed within your brand guidelines. Depending on your requirements, the work can include:

  • 01

    Social media graphics

    Posts, stories, carousels and promotional content for Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and other platforms.

  • 02

    Ad creatives

    Visuals for Meta Ads, Google Display and other platforms, prepared to your campaign objectives and specifications.

  • 03

    Presentations and pitch decks

    Professional presentations, sales decks and investor pitch decks.

  • 04

    Email designs

    Email layouts and newsletter templates for consistent communication with your audience.

  • 05

    Infographics
