WHAT YOU CAN EXPECT

What Your Brand Identity Can Include

Every project is scoped to your business. Depending on your requirements, your finished identity can include:

  • 01

    Logo

    Your logo, supplied in the formats agreed for your project.

  • 02

    Color palette

    A defined set of brand colors, so your identity looks the same wherever it appears.

  • 03

    Typography

    Typefaces selected to suit your brand and keep your communication consistent.

  • 04

    Visual elements

    Supporting graphic elements and imagery direction that extend your identity beyond the logo.

  • 05

    Brand guidelines

    Practical guidance on using your identity across digital platforms, marketing materials and business communications.

  • 06

    Brand assets and files

    Supporting brand assets and, where agreed in the project scope, editable design files.

We confirm the deliverables, file formats and timeline with you before the project starts.
