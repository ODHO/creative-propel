WHAT YOU CAN EXPECT

What a Motion Project Can Include

Every project is scoped to your message and channels. Depending on your requirements, the work can include:

  • 01

    Animated logo

    Your existing logo animated, or a new animation concept that reflects your brand identity.

  • 02

    Motion graphics

    Animated text, transitions, visual effects and graphic elements for your content.

  • 03

    Edited video

    Raw footage edited into polished video, with pacing, transitions, color correction and sound.

  • 04

    Social media videos

    Short-form and vertical videos for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and other channels.

  • 05

    Explainer and promotional videos

    Clear videos that explain your offering, introduce your business or showcase your products.

  • 06

    Storyboards and final files

    A planned visual sequence before production, and platform-ready files in the formats and dimensions you need.

We confirm the formats, dimensions and timeline with you before the project starts.
