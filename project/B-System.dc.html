Animation principles

01 · LIGHT LEADS

The orb moves first and content follows it. Wherever the light goes, attention goes.

02 · FOCUS PULL

Elements arrive from soft blur and slight scale (0.96 → 1), like a camera finding focus.

03 · ORBIT, DON'T SLIDE

Selections rotate along curved paths. Page changes pass through the portal.

04 · TOKENS
