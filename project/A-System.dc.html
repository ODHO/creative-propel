Animation principles

01 · DIRECTION

Everything moves forward: left to right, bottom to top, or along 45°. Nothing drifts aimlessly.

02 · MASKED, NOT FADED

Text rises line by line from behind a mask; images open along the diagonal cut. That reads as print being revealed.

03 · ONE HERO PER VIEW

A single orchestrated moment per screen; everything else is quiet. Scroll is tied to the Propel Line only.

04 · TOKENS
