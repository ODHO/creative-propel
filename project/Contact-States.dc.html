CONTACT PAGE · FORM STATES

Enquiry Form States

Design examples only. The canvas form is a prototype.It is not connected to any email system, form plugin or database. No enquiry is ever sent from the canvas, and no automatic confirmation email is configured. On the Contact page itself, pressing Send Enquiry only checks the fields and shows a “Prototype only” notice. It never shows the success state. Bracketed values such as [entered name] stand for whatever the visitor typed.

03 · SendingDESIGN EXAMPLE

While the enquiry is being sent. The button is disabled to prevent a second submission, and screen readers hear “Sending your enquiry”. With reduced motion, the spinner stays still.

Sending your enquiry…

04 · Successful submissionDESIGN EXAMPLE · NOT A REAL SUBMISSION

Shown ONLY after a real backend confirms the enquiry was received. The form is replaced and focus moves to the heading. Nothing on the canvas is sent, and no confirmation email is configured.

Thank you, [first name]. Your enquiry has been sent.

We've received your project details and will review them. If you'd like to add anything in the meantime, email us at info@creativepropel.com.

If sending fails. The message is announced, everything the visitor entered is kept, and they can try again or email directly.
