Introduction

This Cookies Policy explains how Creative Propel ("Company," "we," "our," or "us") may use cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website at https://creativepropel.com/.

It explains what cookies are, why websites use them, how they may be used on our website, and how you can manage your preferences.

For information about how we collect, use and protect personal information more generally, please read our Privacy Policy.

If you have questions about this Cookies Policy, contact us at info@creativepropel.com.

1.What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites may place on your computer, mobile phone or other device when you visit them.

They help websites remember information about your visit, support certain functions, understand how visitors interact with pages and improve the browsing experience.

Some cookies are temporary and are removed when you close your browser. Others remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them.

2.Why Websites Use Cookies

Cookies and similar technologies can serve different purposes, depending on how a website is configured.

These purposes may include:

  • Helping essential website features operate correctly.
  • Remembering preferences and settings.
  • Understanding how visitors navigate and use website pages.
  • Monitoring website performance and identifying technical issues.
  • Supporting marketing and advertising activities, where applicable.

The cookies active on a website depend on the features, tools and third-party services that the website uses.

3.Types of Cookies

Depending on the technologies implemented on our website, cookies may fall into the following categories:

Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies support essential website functions, such as security, navigation and basic functionality. Without them, certain parts of a website may not work properly.

Functional Cookies

These cookies may remember choices and preferences, such as language or other settings, to provide a more consistent browsing experience.

Analytics and Performance Cookies

These cookies may collect information about how visitors use a website, such as which pages they visit and whether they encounter errors. This information can help website owners understand performance and improve their websites.

Advertising and Marketing Cookies

Where advertising or marketing technologies are implemented, these cookies may help measure advertising performance, understand visitor interests or deliver more relevant marketing.

Not all of these categories are necessarily active on our website. The cookies in use depend on the services and technologies implemented at the time of your visit.

4.First-Party and Third-Party Cookies

First-party cookies are set directly by the website you visit. They may support website functionality, preferences and performance.

Third-party cookies are set by services provided by other organisations that may be integrated into a website. These services may include analytics, embedded content, advertising or other external features.

Third-party providers operate under their own privacy and cookie practices. We encourage you to review their policies where relevant.

6.Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to view, block or delete cookies and configure how websites store them.

Please note that blocking or deleting certain cookies may affect website functionality or prevent some features from working as intended.

Where a cookie preference or consent tool is available on our website, you can use it to manage the categories of cookies that require your consent.

Where applicable law requires consent before non-essential cookies or similar technologies are used, we will seek that consent before activating them.

7.How Long Cookies Remain on Your Device

The duration of a cookie depends on its purpose and configuration.

Session cookies are generally removed when you close your browser.

Persistent cookies remain on your device until they expire or are deleted.

The retention period for individual cookies varies depending on the technology and service responsible for setting them.
