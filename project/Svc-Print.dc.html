WHAT YOU CAN EXPECT

What a Print Design Project Can Include

Every project is scoped to your materials and designed to match your brand. Depending on your requirements, the work can include:

  • 01

    Packaging and labels

    Packaging concepts, product labels, box designs and other packaging visuals.

  • 02

    Print collateral

    Brochures, flyers, posters, menus and promotional materials with clear layouts.

  • 03

    Business stationery

    Branded business cards, letterheads, envelopes and other everyday stationery.

  • 04

    Publications

    Structured layouts for books, magazines, catalogs, company profiles and annual reports.

  • 05

    Event materials

    Banners, event graphics and promotional displays for exhibitions, conferences and brand activations.

  • 06

    Print-ready files

    Final files prepared to the required dimensions, bleed, resolution and printing specifications.

We confirm the formats, printing specifications and timeline with you before the project starts. Printing itself can be discussed separately.
