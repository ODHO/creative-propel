How facts and assets were treated

  • A fact is listed as “verified” only when the client’s own site states it, and every fact has a source link.
  • The site’s current appearance or features are not treated as proof of what Creative Propel built. Footer credits are noted, not relied on.
  • Product photography, lifestyle photography and client-owned logos are flagged as not Creative Propel work.
  • No images were downloaded, embedded, altered or reused. Image filenames and addresses are recorded for reference only.
